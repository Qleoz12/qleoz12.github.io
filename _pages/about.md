## About Me

### me

This is me, a person who sees life as a constant learning process. Over the years, I’ve taken different roles—student, developer, traveler—but one thing has stayed the same: my curiosity. I believe that every challenge, every new experience, is a chance to grow, and that mindset defines who I am today.

### W’s Qts?

I like to think about my journey in terms of questions—the W’s. Who am I becoming? What do I want to build? Why do I work so hard? Where am I heading? When should I take action? And how will I achieve it? These questions don’t always have clear answers, but they keep me moving forward with purpose.

### Journey in Tech

My path in technology began with curiosity. I started small, exploring how code could solve everyday problems. Over time, those experiments grew into bigger projects and eventually into a career. For me, tech is more than a job—it’s the journey of turning ideas into real solutions and constantly improving through practice.

### TECH

Technology is my passion because it connects creativity with problem-solving. I enjoy building systems, automating processes, and collaborating with others through code. It’s not just about computers; it’s about people, ideas, and possibilities. Tech is the tool that allows me to create and make a meaningful impact in the world.

### COUNTRY

I am from Colombia, a country known for its culture, resilience, and strong communities. Growing up there taught me to adapt, to work hard, and to always find opportunities even in tough situations. Even though I live abroad now, my country gives me strength and reminds me of the values that shape my story.

### HOBBY

Outside of work and studies, my hobbies are what keep me balanced and creative. I enjoy reading, exploring, and learning new things—sometimes languages, sometimes skills. Hobbies are a way to recharge, but also to see life from different perspectives. They remind me that growth isn’t only professional—it’s also personal.
